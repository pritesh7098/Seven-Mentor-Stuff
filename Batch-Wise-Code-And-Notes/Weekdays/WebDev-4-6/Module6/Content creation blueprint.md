# 16. Recording Basics

Basic setup:

```text id="koecjj"
Window / Light
      ↓
    You
      ↓
    Phone
```

The light should generally illuminate your face rather than sit behind you.

Camera:

- Approximately eye level
- Stable
- Reasonable framing
- Avoid extreme angles

---

# 17. Audio > Camera

A simple principle:

> **Poor video can sometimes be tolerated. Poor audio is much harder to tolerate.**

But don't immediately buy expensive audio equipment.

First test:

1. Record on phone.
2. Listen using headphones.
3. Check whether every word is clear.

If yes:

> Good enough for Week 1.
